What we collect

We collect information you give us when you join or apply, such as your name, address, Social Security number and income, and information about your transactions with us. We also collect basic technical information when you visit our website or use our app, such as your device type and the pages you view.

How we use it

We use your information to open and service your accounts, process transactions, prevent fraud, meet legal requirements and improve our services. We do not sell your personal information.

How we share it

We share information only as needed to run your accounts, with service providers who work for us under contract, with regulators and as the law requires. We do not share your information with outside companies for their own marketing.

How we protect it

  • Information is encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • Access is limited to employees who need it to serve you.
  • We monitor our systems around the clock.
